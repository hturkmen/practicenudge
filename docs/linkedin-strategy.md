### Strategy for Finding First 20 Leads

**Step 1: Low-hanging fruit (5 leads)**
- Search LinkedIn for "MTD" + "accountant" + "UK" in posts from last 30 days
- These people are already thinking/talking about MTD
- Engage with their posts first, then connect

**Step 2: Professional bodies (5 leads)**
- Search members of ICAEW, ACCA, AAT who mention "practice owner" or "sole practitioner"
- Filter by UK location, small company size
- Look at LinkedIn groups: "UK Accountants", "Making Tax Digital", "Small Practice Owners"

**Step 3: Content engagers (5 leads)**
- Find people who've engaged with MTD-related content (liked, commented)
- Check followers of AccountingWEB, ICAEW, Xero, QuickBooks UK pages
- These are active LinkedIn users more likely to respond

**Step 4: Local/regional search (5 leads)**
- Search by specific UK regions (start with areas you know)
- Look for practices with websites but small teams (1-5 people)
- Cross-reference with Google Maps "accountants near [city]" to find practice owners on LinkedIn

### Engagement Before Outreach

**Do this before sending connection requests:**
1. Like 2-3 of their recent posts
2. Leave a thoughtful comment on one post (not about PracticeNudge)
3. Wait 1-2 days
4. Send connection request

This increases acceptance rate from ~20% to ~40%+.

### Posting Best Practices

- **Frequency:** 2x per week minimum
- **Best times:** Tuesday–Thursday, 7:30–8:30am or 12:00–1:00pm UK time
- **Format mix:** Text-only (60%), text + image (30%), document/carousel (10%)
- **Hashtags:** 3-5 per post, mix of broad (#Accounting) and niche (#MTDITSA)
- **Engagement:** Reply to every comment within 2 hours
- **Personal vs Company:** Post from personal profile AND share to company page
