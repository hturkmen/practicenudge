SEO compounds over 6–12 months. You need 10 paying customers in 60 days to:

- Generate case studies and testimonials

The UK accounting community is small and interconnected. 10 happy firms will generate
more growth than 10,000 blog visitors.

## Channel 1: LinkedIn Outreach (Primary — 50% of effort)


### Target Profile

- UK-based sole practitioners or small practice owners (1–5 staff)
- Managing 20–200 clients
- Active on LinkedIn (posted or engaged in last 30 days)
- Mentions MTD, self-assessment, or practice management in profile
- ICAEW, ACCA, or AAT qualified

### Daily Cadence (30 minutes/day)

| Day | Activity | Volume |
|-----|----------|--------|
| Mon | Research 10 new prospects, engage with their content | 10 profiles |
| Tue | Send 10 connection requests with personalised note | 10 requests |
| Wed | First messages to accepted connections | 5–8 messages |
| Thu | Follow-ups to non-responders (3-day rule) | 5–8 follow-ups |
| Fri | Post content, engage with comments, DM warm leads | 1 post + engagement |

### Connection Request Script (300 chars)

**Version A (Direct):**
```
Hi [Name], I've built a simple MTD client tracking tool for small practices —
no more spreadsheet chaos. Saw you're running a practice in [City] and thought
it might be relevant. Happy to share a free tracker template either way.
```

**Version B (Soft):**
```
Hi [Name], I work with small UK practices preparing for MTD ITSA. Always good
to connect with others in the space. I've put together a free MTD readiness
tracker — happy to share if useful for your practice.
```

**Version C (Engagement-first):**
```
Hi [Name], really liked your post about [specific topic]. I'm building tools
for small practices managing MTD — would be great to connect and hear how
you're approaching it with your clients.
```

### First Message (After Connection Accepted)

```
Thanks for connecting, [Name]!

Quick question — how are you currently tracking which clients are MTD-ready
and which still need onboarding?

I ask because I've been speaking to a lot of sole practitioners, and the

No pressure either way. If the spreadsheet helps, that's a win.

Cheers,
Halil
```

### Follow-Up Message (Day 3, No Reply)

```
Hi [Name], just a quick follow-up — no worries if you're busy.

The free MTD tracker is here if useful:
https://www.practicenudge.com/mtd

If you ever want to chat about how other small practices are approaching
```

## Conversion Tactics

### The "Free Tracker → Pilot → Paid" Ladder

```
Step 1: Give away the free MTD tracker (Google Sheet)
        → Proves you understand their problem
        → Gets your name in their workflow
        → Costs them nothing

Step 2: After 2–3 weeks, they hit spreadsheet limitations
        → "Want this automated? Free pilot, no card."
        → Low friction upgrade

Step 3: After 4–6 weeks of pilot usage
        → They've imported clients, set up reminders
        → Switching cost is now real
        → "Keep everything for £39/month"
```

### Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I already use a spreadsheet" | "Great — our free tracker is better than most. Try it. If you outgrow it, we're here." |
| "I use Sage/Xero for everything" | "PracticeNudge works alongside Sage/Xero — it tracks readiness, not bookkeeping. Different job." |
| "I only have 20 clients" | "Perfect — that's exactly who we built this for. Enterprise tools are overkill for you." |
| "I'll wait until I need it" | "Phase 1 is live now. Q1 deadline is August. The practices starting now are the calm ones." |
| "Is my data safe?" | "UK-hosted (Vercel EU), GDPR compliant, encrypted. We don't touch HMRC or client financials." |
| "What if you shut down?" | "Fair question. You can export everything as CSV anytime. No lock-in." |

### Pricing Psychology

- **Pilot:** Free for 8 weeks (removes all risk)
- **Early bird:** £29/month for first 10 customers (creates urgency + reward)
- **Standard:** £39/month (anchors against the early bird discount)
- **Annual:** £349/year (save £119 — for committed practices)

Frame the cost:
- "Less than 1 billable hour per month"
- "Saves 5+ hours of chasing per week"
- "Pays for itself if it saves you losing one client to a missed deadline"

## The Mindset Shift

**Stop thinking:** "How do I get traffic to my website?"
**Start thinking:** "How do I get in front of 10 specific practice owners this week?"

At this stage, every customer should feel hand-picked. You should know:
- Their name
- Their practice name
- How many clients they have
- What software they use
- Whether they've posted about MTD

This is not scalable. It's not meant to be. It's meant to get you to 10 paying
customers who love the product and tell their friends.

Scale comes after product-market fit. Product-market fit comes from 10 deeply
engaged customers, not 10,000 anonymous website visitors.

## Post-10-Customers: What Changes

Once you have 10 paying firms:

1. **Case studies:** Write 2–3 detailed case studies (with permission)
2. **Testimonials:** Get quotes for the website
3. **Referral programme:** Formalise "1 month free per referral"
4. **SEO investment:** Now the content strategy (Phase 1–10 above) becomes primary
5. **Paid ads:** Test Google Ads on high-intent keywords (£200–500/month)
6. **Partnerships:** Approach Xero/QuickBooks for co-marketing
7. **Pricing confidence:** You now have data on willingness to pay

The SEO strategy document (`docs/seo-strategy-complete.md`) becomes your primary
growth engine from month 4 onwards. But months 1–3 are founder-led sales.
